This site provides independent tracking and analysis of each CFL team's **Negotiation List**.

### What Is a Negotiation List?

From the CFL:
> Each CFL team maintains a Negotiation List of up to 45 players who are currently either unsigned or are playing in the NFL, in another professional league, or in college. Teams hold exclusive CFL negotiating rights with players on their lists, and those players can be added, traded or removed at any time.

Previously, these lists were infamously secretive [until July 2025](https://press.cfl.ca/cfl-negotiation-lists-fully-unveiled) where the league required that they were made public and viewable through the [CFL website](https://www.cfl.ca/negotiation-list/).
